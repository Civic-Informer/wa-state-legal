# Pacific County North District Court — Local Court Rules

**Source:** https://www.courts.wa.gov/court_rules/pdf/LCR/25/DIS/Pacific_County_North_District_Court/LCR_Pacific_North_DIS.pdf  (provenance only — do not refetch)
**Snapshot date:** 2026-05-20  
**Effective date of base rules:** 2025-09-01  

**Fidelity verdict:** PARTIAL. Publisher only publishes Local Court Rule 4 (venue-routing between Pacific North and Pacific South) and an undated emergency Local Court Rule 5 (DUI mandatory appearance). The full base ruleset is not on courts.wa.gov; contact the court clerk for any rule outside these two. The single source PDF is also scanned/OCR'd.

## Caveats

- Only Local Court Rule 4 (venue-routing between Pacific North and Pacific South) and an undated emergency Local Court Rule 5 (DUI mandatory appearance) are published. There is no consolidated base ruleset on courts.wa.gov for this court.
