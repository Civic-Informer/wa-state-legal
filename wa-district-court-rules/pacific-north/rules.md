# Pacific County North District Court — Local Court Rules

**Source:** https://www.courts.wa.gov/court_rules/pdf/LCR/25/DIS/Pacific_County_North_District_Court/LCR_Pacific_North_DIS.pdf  
**Snapshot date:** 2026-05-20  
**Effective date of base rules:** 2025-09-01  

LOCAL COURT RULE NO. 4

All Civil Infractions, including Traffic Infractions, Criminal Citations and
Criminal Complaints shall be filed in the district in which the underlying
facts occurred to wit;

Offenses occurring in the following voting districts shall be filed in Pacific
County South District Court:

      Chinook, Ilwaco 1R, Ilwaco 2R, Ilwaco, Klipsan, Long Beach, Nahcotta,
      Naselle, Nemah, Ocean Park 1, Ocean Park 2, Oysterville, Pioneer,
      Seaview 1, Seaview 2.

Offenses occurring in the following voting districts shall be filed in Pacific
County North District Court:

      Baleville, Bay Center, Ekland Park, Frances, Menlo, North River,
      Raymond Ward 2, Raymond Ward 3, Smith Creek, South Bend 1R, South
      Bend 2R, South Fork, Willapa, Raymond Ward 1P1, Raymond Ward 1P2,
      Raymond Ward 1P3, Raymond Ward 2, Raymond Ward 3, South Bend 1,
      South Bend 2, South Bend 3.

Any Citation filed in violation of this rule may be dismissed without prejudice
or transferred without notice to the correct district.

Effective September 1, 2018                   .

                             LOCAL COURT RULE #5

                  WAIVER OF MANDATORY APPEARANCE

Pursuant to R.C.W. 46.61.50571 relating to mandatory appearances in
DUI/Physical Control cases.

The Court hereby waives the requirement of a mandatory court appearance on the
next judicial day if served with a citation or complaint.

The Court further requires a mandatory court appearance on the first Tuesday
following arrest at 10:00 AM.

The Court deems an emergency exists and this rule shall become effective
immediately.
