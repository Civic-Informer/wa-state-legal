Rule 4.    Procedure at Contested Hearings




                               Rule 4.
                   Procedure at Contested Hearings


     Speed Measuring Device Experts: When any speed measuring device
expert is required to testify in a contested infraction hearing, the
expert may testify by telephone, unless otherwise ordered by the Court.
The party required to produce such evidence shall be responsible for
arranging the expert's telephonic testimony and advising the Court Clerk
prior to the scheduled time for the contested hearing.
