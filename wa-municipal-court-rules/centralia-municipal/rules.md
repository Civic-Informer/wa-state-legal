                                             Rule 3

                                    Release of the Accused

(a)    Any person arrested for the following offenses shall be held in jail, without bail, pending
       the first appearance unless otherwise ordered by the Court:

       (1)    Any offense classified under Section 10.99, chapter 9A.36, or chapter 9A.46 of the
              Revised Code of Washington as Domestic Violence; or

       (2)    A violation of RCW 46.61.502 (Driving Under the Influence), RCW 46.61.503
              (Driver Under 21 Consuming Alcohol), or RCW 46.61.504 (Physical Control of
              Vehicle Under the Influence), when the person has previously been convicted of or
              had a deferred prosecution granted for one or more of any of these offenses.

(ob)   Any person arrested for the following offenses shall be held in jail, subject to bail in the
       amount of $1,000.00 cash or appearance bond, pending the first appearance unless
       otherwise ordered by the Court:

       (1)    A violation of RCW 9A.56.050 (Theft 3"); or

       (2)    A violation of RCW 9A.52.070 (Criminal Trespass 1°) or RCW 9A.52.080
              (Criminal Trespass 2").
